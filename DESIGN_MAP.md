# Research Overlay Design Map

## AI Macro

1. Snapshot: deterministic headline, Expansion, Constraints, and This week; no generated synthesis sentence
2. Snapshot context: first-class market, finance, infrastructure, energy, adaptation, and validation domains with explicit coverage; primary-source references appear below the weekly rollup
3. Regime board: AEI, ADI, Power Stress, and Business Adaptation
4. Zero-centered gap comparison: speculation, validation, industrial, and power capacity
5. Parallel ADI, economic-validation, and power component panels
6. Component observations and normalization detail

## Market

1. Dynamic loaded-universe label: configured/loaded sector and ticker-slot counts
2. Current assessment strip including adjusted Most Concentrated sector basket
3. Positioning and rotation views
4. Full sortable sector matrix
5. Selector-driven dossier
6. AEI factor structure and trading-pressure structure
7. Full company table and ticker controls

## Finance

1. Funding Profile: four comparable funding cards and retained history
2. Debt Markets: overall, investment-grade, and high-yield CMDI readings with ten-year history
3. Borrower Strain: reading, source state, trend statistics, history, components
4. Lender Strain: same structure, kept economically distinct
5. NFCI/ANFCI confirmation panel

## Infrastructure

1. Evidence-graded facility registry combining observed IM3 records and curated primary-evidence projects
2. Homogeneous bubble-size selector for count, area, capacity, electricity, withdrawal, or consumption; unavailable values remain outlined
3. Facility evidence table with status, location precision, field-level capacity/power/water values, grade, date, and source
4. Buildout chart directly below the summary readings: data centers and the broader computer/electronic/electrical manufacturing category
5. U.S. Infrastructure Expenditure: communication, highway, transportation, and public water-supply construction without AI attribution

## Energy

1. Energy supply: Henry Hub, WTI, coal production, and renewable-power output
2. Electricity cost: national commercial and industrial average retail prices with 12-month changes
3. Power production: output, capacity, utilization, and Power Stress
4. Grid capacity: Power Capacity Gap, output growth, capacity growth, utilization, and eight-year response history
5. AI energy demand: construction, capital deployment, and commercial-load evidence

## Adaptation

1. Current and expected employer-business AI use
2. National adoption trajectory with 95% intervals from published standard errors
3. Expected Adoption Gap shown without unsupported difference-estimate uncertainty
4. Adoption breadth across major industries with 95% intervals
5. Explicit boundary between adoption and realized productivity, ROI, labor, and institutional effects

## Evidence

1. Purpose and analytical boundary
2. Product readings, source/fallback state, and versions
3. Component, cohort, and facility-field coverage
4. Complete metric registry
5. Raw FRED, Energy, Debt Markets, Infrastructure, Adaptation, and EDGAR observations
